About

Dr. Yingnan Shi is a Lecturer at the Centre for Business Data Analytics (CBDA), The University of Western Australia (UWA). Before joining UWA, he was affiliated with CSIRO and The Australian National University (ANU), where he worked across research, teaching and interdisciplinary projects in information systems, artificial intelligence, management and digital technologies.

His research sits at the intersection of AI, Information Systems, applied psychology and management, with a particular interest in how people interact, collaborate and make decisions with artificial intelligence. His work also examines digital platforms, knowledge systems and the organisational implications of emerging technologies.

Before entering academia, Yingnan gained experience across telecommunications, banking, NGOs and medical equipment manufacturing. He earned the Certified Information Systems Auditor (CISA) credential from ISACA in 2015 and is a member of the Association for Information Systems (AIS).
