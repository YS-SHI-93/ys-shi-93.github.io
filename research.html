Research

My work spans artificial intelligence, Information Systems, applied psychology, management, knowledge systems and digital platforms.

Human–AI Interaction & Applied Psychology

I study how people interact and collaborate with AI systems, including how timing, communication style, task design and user perceptions influence human–AI outcomes. This stream bridges technically oriented Information Systems research with human-centred behavioural research.

Human–AI collaboration Decision making Applied psychology

Knowledge Systems & Semantic Technologies

My earlier work includes Semantic Web applications, knowledge graphs and question-answering-based knowledge evaluation, focusing on how information can be structured, represented, retrieved and evaluated in complex digital environments.

Semantic Web Knowledge graphs QA systems

Technology, Management & Organisational Behaviour

This stream examines how emerging technologies affect employees and organisations, including AI-enabled work, personality assessment, knowledge sharing, leadership, psychological empowerment and technology-enabled organisational change.

Future of work Empowerment HRM

Digital Platforms, Marketing & Information Economics

I study behaviour on digital platforms including Reddit, Twitter/X, Instagram, Facebook, Coursera, edX and livestreaming platforms, using quantitative and qualitative methods to investigate online communities, digital consumption and platform-mediated behaviour.

Digital platforms E-commerce Online communities
